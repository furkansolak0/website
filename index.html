Spreadsheets

One system

The platform

Two apps. One match feed.

Organisers run the league from one app and broadcast from another. Both read and write the same match data in real time — a goal logged on the camera screen appears in the league app instantly.

01
Flutter · Android

Prolig League

Everything a league organiser needs, from the first fixture to the season report.

  • Teams, squads and 7-a-side lineups with formation view
  • Live scoring from an operator screen
  • Automatic standings, top scorers and player stats
  • PDF match reports and full season reports
  • Ready-to-post social graphics: results, fixtures, tables
  • Monthly bonus-point system for teams
02
Android · Native

Baykul Broadcaster

Turns a phone into a broadcast camera and a live scoring console — streaming straight to YouTube Live.

  • Log goals, cards and substitutions mid-stream — without leaving the camera view
  • Every event syncs instantly to the Prolig league app: score, standings, player stats
  • Real-time score and lineup overlays on the stream
  • Direct YouTube Live broadcast setup from the app
  • Stream health monitoring with automatic retry
