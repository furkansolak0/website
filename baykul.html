Features

A camera and a scoring console in one

One person with one phone can film the match and keep the score at the same time.

Broadcast

  • Direct YouTube Live broadcast setup from the app
  • Real-time score and lineup overlays on the stream
  • Stream health monitoring with automatic retry
  • Runs on a standard Android phone and a tripod

Match logging

  • Log goals, cards and substitutions mid-stream, without leaving the camera view
  • Every event syncs instantly to Prolig: score, standings, player stats
  • Start the match, half-time and second half from the same panel
  • Overlays driven by the same data as the league app
